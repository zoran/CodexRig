# Task Quality

Own evidence selection and acceptance at a real slice/outcome boundary.
[Instructions](../../../instructions.md) own authority, coordination, housekeeping and publication.
Review-only requests stay read-only; repairs need existing implementation authority.

1. Recover accepted scope, completion condition, changed owners/consumers and risks. Inspect
   `pnpm verify:changed -- --print-plan`; reuse valid results and select only necessary gaps.
   Reviews, audits, handoffs and resume are not check triggers. Independent successes survive
   failure.
2. Compose `$system-coherence` evidence once for nontrivial code/integration. Add relevant security,
   UI/UX, native-language, raster-image or search review only for changed surfaces; do not duplicate
   their concern. Critical internal contracts must exercise shipped code and the consumer validator,
   not unconditional mocks. Distinguish static, fixture, rendered, native and target observations.
3. Resolve material in-scope findings at their root, then repeat only affected evidence. An audit
   does not extend scope through optional improvements or restart accepted work. Use the canonical
   [course check](../../../instructions.md#long-session-course-checks) to reassess repeated failure
   or disproportionate effort.
4. Reconcile [documentation currency](../../../instructions.md#documentation-currency), including
   protected-owner corrections and semantic preservation of unique requirements. Structural checks
   alone cannot prove preservation. Inspect current manifest, links, dead paths and work context.
5. Audit remaining acceptance and whole-repository coherence/efficiency, reconcile owned agents/jobs
   and residue, then run worktree inventory and canonical settlement under
   [closure](../../../instructions.md#completed-goal-closure-and-repository-housekeeping). Preserve
   active/private/unrelated state; preservation alone is not completion.
6. Publication requires its own authority and
   [delegated release](../../../instructions.md#delegated-release) gates. Assemble complete
   candidate coverage once from valid and missing units; local handoff does not require a
   preliminary full suite. Later edits reopen only affected evidence. Critical capacity follows the
   canonical terminal drain, never fabricated completion.

Continue unfinished authorized work after checkpoints. Final handoff states concrete changes,
observed evidence/review, material limits and any actual remaining action. Create no review,
completion or process-history document and invent no post-acceptance work.
