# Task Quality

At an accepted outcome or slice boundary, compare the requested result with the changed owners,
their consumers and observed behavior. A standalone review stays read-only.

1. Inspect `pnpm verify:changed -- --print-plan`, reuse valid results and fill only relevant gaps.
   Exercise critical contracts through the shipped implementation and its consumer validator.
   Distinguish static, fixture, rendered, native and target evidence.
2. Compose `$system-coherence` for nontrivial integration. Add security, UI/UX, language, image or
   search review for the affected surface. Resolve authorized blocking findings at their owner and
   recheck the affected path. Apply
   [course checks](../../../instructions.md#long-session-course-checks) if efforts repeat without
   new evidence.
3. Reconcile [documentation](../../../instructions.md#documentation-currency), including the
   manifest, links and protected-owner corrections. Check that unique requirements survive
   consolidation. Follow
   [closure](../../../instructions.md#completed-goal-closure-and-repository-housekeeping) for owned
   jobs, residue, worktrees and the final account of results and limits.

For authorized publication assemble full candidate coverage once and follow
[delegated release](../../../instructions.md#delegated-release) and the applicable
[delivery gates](../../../instructions.md#continuous-delivery-and-manual-testing). Local completion
ends the accepted task. Keep the handoff in the conversation.
